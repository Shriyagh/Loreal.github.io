01 · Meet the product

Six parts. One quiet
piece of engineering.

Select a part to see what it does. The sleeve, pump and base stay with you; only the cartridge is replaced.

  • Two separate channels end in two side-by-side outlets, so the SPF base and your booster leave the bottle as two ribbons. The nozzle seals itself between uses.
  • A quarter turn of the pump head opens or locks the bottle. The base also twists a quarter turn to hold the cartridge in place, and only clicks shut when the cartridge is seated correctly.
  • A slim window shows both serum levels at a glance. The fittings at the top and bottom of the cartridge stay hidden behind the sleeve.
  • A thin, anodised sleeve over a light recycled-plastic frame. Keep it for years; it's the part you never throw away. A green marking appears in its slot when the cartridge is locked.
  • The left chamber of every cartridge holds the same broad-spectrum SPF 50+ PA++++ base with vitamin E. Every press delivers the full sunscreen dose.
  • The right chamber holds your booster: one of five, chosen for your skin type or climate. Both chambers form one frosted, airless cartridge that you swap as a single piece.

02 · How to use it

Your morning,
in five movements.

Scroll, or use the step controls, to watch each step on the bottle.

STEP 01

Insert your cartridge.

Your starter kit comes with the bottle and one cartridge. It only fits one way.

  1. Hold the cartridge with the shaped tab facing up.
  2. Line the tab up with the slot inside the sleeve.
  3. Slide it in, then twist the base a quarter turn until it clicks.
How you know it's right: the green marking printed on the cartridge appears in the slot near the base.
STEP 02

Twist to unlock.

Turn the pump head a quarter turn. The twin outlets swing forward and the nozzle opens.

  1. Hold the sleeve in one hand.
  2. Turn the pump head a quarter turn in the direction of the arrow.
Ready: the outlets face forward and the small dot on the pump head shows open.
STEP 03

Dispense your serum.

Press the pump twice. Two ribbons come out side by side: the cream SPF base and your coloured booster.

  1. Hold your fingertips under the outlets.
  2. Press down fully, twice, for one full-face dose.
Same protection every time: each press gives a fixed SPF dose plus a fixed booster dose.
STEP 04

Blend and apply.

Bring the two ribbons together with your fingertips, then smooth evenly over your face and neck.

  1. Blend in small circles until it's one even colour.
  2. Apply as the last step of your morning routine.
  3. Reapply through the day if you're in strong sun.
The booster's sheer tint comes from the formula itself and disappears as you blend.
STEP 05

Twist back to lock.

Turn the pump head back a quarter turn. The outlets swing away and the nozzle seals shut, ready for your bag.

  1. Turn the pump head back against the arrow.
  2. Feel the click: it's locked and sealed.
Locked: the pump can't be pressed, so nothing leaks or dries on the tip.

03 · Time for a refill

Keep the sleeve.
Swap the cartridge.

A new cartridge clicks in within seconds. Watch which parts stay and which part is replaced.

STEP 01

Check the level window.

As you use it, both serum levels drop in the window on the front of the sleeve.

  1. Look at the window each morning.
  2. When the levels reach the bottom mark, it's time for a new cartridge.
Both chambers are sized to run out together, so you never replace half a cartridge.
STEP 02

Unlock the bottle.

Twist the base a quarter turn the other way. It unclicks and comes away from the sleeve.

  1. Make sure the pump head is locked first.
  2. Twist the base a quarter turn and pull it gently down.
STEP 03

Remove the empty cartridge.

The cartridge slides out of the sleeve in one piece. Its valves close automatically, so nothing drips.

  1. Slide the cartridge down and out.
  2. Return the empty to the in-store collection bin for recycling.
Cartridges aren't opened or topped up. Each one is sealed and swapped whole for a new refill.
STEP 04

Insert a new cartridge.

Peel the seals off a fresh cartridge, line up the shaped tab and slide it in until it stops.

  1. Remove both peel-off seals from the outlets.
  2. Tab up, aligned with the slot, slide it fully in.
STEP 05

Twist and secure.

Put the base back on and twist a quarter turn until it clicks. The green marking shows the cartridge is locked.

  1. Push the base up onto the sleeve.
  2. Twist until it clicks and the green marking appears.

Keep the outer sleeve. Replace the cartridge. Less packaging, more thoughtful skincare.
